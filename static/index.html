Anemia screening from an eye photo

Research prototype for triage, not a diagnosis. Only a blood test measures hemoglobin.

  1. Face a window or a bright light, no flash.
  2. Pull the lower eyelid down with one finger to show the pink inner part.
  3. Place the pink part inside the oval and hold still: the photo takes itself.
